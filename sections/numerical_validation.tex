\section{Numerical Checks}
\label{sec:numerical}

The algebra in \cref{sec:canonical,sec:scalar} does not need a data set.
This section does two smaller things.
It checks that the code evaluates the identities, and it compares \cref{prop:plugin} with bivariate-normal samples.
The paired estimates come from the empirical pipeline.
Provenance is \texttt{validation\_inputs/\_manifest.csv}.
The simulation is \texttt{scripts/run\_pef\_geometry\_numerical\_model.m}.

\label{sec:val_checks}

It does not test the formula.

A separate bootstrap comparison of $(\hat\tau,\hat\rho)$ under the two label orders is not reported.
If the clouds keep the sign of $\hat\tau$, they are mirror images whenever $\tau\neq 0$, so a difference is expected.
If the test uses $|\hat\tau|$, the clouds agree by construction.
Neither outcome is evidence about \cref{thm:involution}.

The label swap does change the predictive coordinates.
Write $V = (1+\kappa)/\eta$, $d_{\mathrm{rel}} = \delta/\sqrt{\Var(X_{\mathrm{A}}-X_{\mathrm{B}})}$, and $r_{\mathrm{A}} = \Corr(X_{\mathrm{A}}, X_{\mathrm{A}}-X_{\mathrm{B}})$.
At fixed $\delta/\sigma_{\mathrm{A}}$, the swap multiplies $d_{\mathrm{rel}}$ by $\sqrt{\kappa}$ and sends $r_{\mathrm{A}}$ to $-r_{\mathrm{B}}$.
On the 86 sports indicators the factor $\sqrt{\kappa}$ runs from $0.761$ to $1.160$.
One labelling can therefore sit $24\%$ away from the other.
The residuals of $d_{\mathrm{rel}}(1/\kappa) = \sqrt{\kappa}\, d_{\mathrm{rel}}(\kappa)$ and of $r_{\mathrm{B}} = -r_{\mathrm{A}}(1/\kappa)$ are at most $1.1\times 10^{-15}$ (\texttt{scripts/outputs/involution\_equivariance\_check.csv}).
Again, that is a check of the code.
The $24\%$ gap is the reason the cell split on $\kappa = 1$ still matters for a comparison that uses $X_{\mathrm{A}}$.

Finite differences of $\log\hat\eta$ on the 24 sports indicators with $\hat\rho>0$ recover $\hat\eta-1$ and $\hat\eta(\hat\eta-1)$ to $10^{-6}$.
That is the step size of the difference.
It is the same closed form as \cref{sec:latent_family}.

\subsection{Latent Family and Plug-in}
\label{sec:val_geometry_model}

Block~C of the simulation draws the latent family \cref{eq:geo_family} and forms its maximum-likelihood estimator.
On 36 cells of the positive-$u$ half-strip, with $n = 200$ and 400 trials, the median of $n\,\Var(\hat\psi)$ is $1.011$.
The median of $\Var(\hat\eta)$ divided by $\eta(\eta-1)/n$ is $0.997$.
Both ratios have a between-cell coefficient of variation below $0.08$ (\texttt{geometry\_psi\_sampling.csv}).
This is \cref{eq:psi_clt,eq:var_eta_latent}.
It is not a statement about paired observations.

Block~D draws bivariate-normal pairs at the same $(\tau,\rho)$ and forms the plug-in $(\hat\tau,\hat\rho)$.
There are 66 cells, with $n = 500$ and 400 trials.
The median of $\Var(\hat u)$ divided by \cref{eq:var_u_plugin} is $1.009$, and every cell falls between $0.86$ and $1.22$ (\texttt{geometry\_bvn\_plugin.csv}).
\Cref{fig:plugin} shows the comparison.
On the same draws, $n\,\Var(\hat\psi)$ runs from $0.29$ to $47$.
The median is $14.9$ on the cells with $|u|<0.15$, and $3.9$ on the cells with $u>0.5$.
The plug-in $\hat\psi$ is least stable near independence.
\Cref{eq:psi_clt} does not describe it.

Cross-unit coefficients of variation are not used.
An interval for a mean across indicators reflects how many indicators there are, and how far apart they sit.
It is not the sampling variance of one indicator.
A within-indicator bootstrap of the match series would be the corresponding check on the sports data.
Those series are not in \texttt{validation\_inputs/}.

The sign of $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is not re-estimated here.
The empirical paper reports team-blocked intervals for Quadrant~3.
A pooled mean on every indicator with $\hat\rho<0$ mixes cells, and the indicators from the same matches are dependent.
