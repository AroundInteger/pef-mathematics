\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What May Be Cited}

Three statements are safe to cite from this note.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item The canonical form and the involution (\cref{thm:involution}).
  The involution fixes $\eta$.
  A comparison that uses the labelled margin $X_{\mathrm{A}}$ is not fixed.
  On the sports estimates, $d_{\mathrm{rel}}$ and $r_{\mathrm{A}}$ move by up to $24\%$ under $\kappa\mapsto 1/\kappa$ (\cref{sec:numerical}).
  \item The scalar $u = \rho\,\sech\tau$ is the pairwise intraclass correlation, and $\eta = 1/(1-u)$ (\cref{eq:u_def}).
  \item Under bivariate normality, $\Var(\hat u)$ depends on $\tau$ (\cref{prop:plugin}).
  The latent-family law $\Var(\hat\psi)\approx 1/n$ does not apply to the plug-in.
  Fisher's $z$ is the equal-variance case.
  There is no regime change at $\rho = 0$ in $\eta = 1/(1-u)$.
  That map is analytic at $u = 0$.
\end{enumerate}

The empirical paper should not cite $\psi$ as a meta-analytic scale, and it should not cite a regime change at $\rho = 0$.
Quadrant~4 in that paper is the cell $\kappa>1$, $\rho<0$.
The sign of $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is reported there from team-blocked intervals.
This note does not re-adjudicate it.
The empirical paper is submitted first.
This note is intended to appear with it.

\subsection{What the Algebra Does Not Show}

An algebraic identity is not confirmed by evaluating it on paired estimates.
The machine-precision residuals in \cref{sec:numerical} check the code.
The bivariate-normal simulation checks \cref{prop:plugin} inside the model that the proposition assumes.
It does not replace a within-indicator bootstrap of the match series.
Those series are not in the summary files used here.

The latent count $N$ is not a measurement.
Its mean $\eta-1$ is not a signal about a match outcome, and its variance is not mutual information.

\subsection{Scope}

The results are for one pair of scalar measurements.
A matrix analogue would need a different argument.
The plug-in expansion is first order.
Coverage for small $n$ is not derived.
The manufacturing estimates in \cref{tab:datasets} use a small number of axes within each cycle, so the correlation in that row is not a long paired sample.
The source name is the one recorded by the empirical pipeline.
It is not re-identified here.
